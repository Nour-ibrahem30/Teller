# Conversion Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how the Teller website will convert visitors into leads and clients, establishing the conversion goals, paths, and optimization approach.

**Status:** Draft — PROPOSED  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-26

---

## Conversion Goals

### PROPOSED Primary Conversion

**Goal:** Contact form submission or direct inquiry

**Why:** Services business requires conversation before engagement

**Target action:** Complete contact form with project details

### PROPOSED Secondary Conversions

| Conversion | Purpose | Value |
|------------|---------|-------|
| Email newsletter signup | Nurture relationship | Medium (TBD) |
| Content download | Lead capture | Medium (TBD) |
| Social follow | Extend relationship | Low |
| Return visit | Engagement | Medium |

## Conversion Elements

### PROPOSED Call-to-Action (CTA) Strategy

#### Primary CTA

**Text (PROPOSED):**
- "Let's Talk" / "خلينا نتكلم"
- "Start Your Story" / "ابدأ قصتك"

**Placement:**
- Homepage hero
- Navigation header (sticky)
- Service page bottoms
- Case study conclusions
- Footer

#### Secondary CTAs

| CTA | Purpose | Location |
|-----|---------|----------|
| "View Our Work" | Move to consideration | Homepage, Services |
| "Learn More" | Deepen engagement | Service cards |
| "Read Case Study" | Build confidence | Work section |
| "Subscribe" | Capture for nurture | Blog, Footer (TBD) |

### Trust Signals

### Friction Reduction

**PROPOSED Approaches:**

| Friction Point | Solution |
|----------------|----------|
| "What will happen if I contact?" | Explain next steps on contact page |
| "Is this too expensive?" | Frame as investment, offer discovery call |
| "Will they understand my business?" | Show relevant case studies |
| "How long will it take?" | Provide process/timeline overview |
| "Can I trust them?" | Display testimonials, logos, results |

## Contact Form Strategy

### PROPOSED Form Design

**Fields:**

| Field | Required | Purpose |
|-------|----------|---------|
| Name | Yes | Personalization |
| Email | Yes | Follow-up |
| Phone | Optional | Alternative contact |
| Company/Brand | Optional | Context |
| Service interest | Optional | Routing |
| Message | Yes | Understanding need |

**Form behavior:**
- Simple, non-intimidating
- Clear purpose explanation
- Expected response time stated
- Confirmation message/page
