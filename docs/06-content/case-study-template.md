# Case Study Template

## Purpose

This document provides a template and guidelines for creating compelling case studies for the Teller website.

**Status:** Draft — Template  
**Last Updated:** 2026-10-26

---

## Case Study Structure

### Section 1: Overview

**Word count:** 50-100 words

| Element | Description | Required |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Title | Engaging project name | Yes |
| Client | Company/individual name | If permitted |
| Industry | Client's sector | Yes |
| Service(s) | Teller services used | Yes |
| Summary | One-sentence impact statement | Yes |

**Template:**

```
[Title]: [Engaging name that hints at the story]

Client: [Name] (or "A leading [industry] company")
Industry: [Industry]
Services: [Service 1, Service 2]

[One sentence describing the transformation or result]
```

### Section 2: The Challenge

**Word count:** 100-200 words

Describe the problem or situation the client faced before Teller.

**Elements:**
- Client context (who they are, what they do)
- Specific challenge or pain point
- Impact of the problem
- Why they needed help

**Template questions:**
- What was the client's situation?
- What specific problem were they facing?
- How was this affecting their business?
- What had they tried before?

### Section 3: The Approach

**Word count:** 150-250 words

Describe Teller's strategy and methodology.

**Elements:**
- Initial discovery/research
- Strategic approach
- Key decisions made
- Creative solutions
- Treller framework application (if relevant)

**Template questions:**
- How did Teller approach the problem?
- What insights were discovered?
- What strategy was developed?
- What made this approach unique?

### Section 4: The Solution

**Word count:** 150-250 words

Describe what was created or delivered.

**Elements:**
- Deliverables produced
- Key messages or stories developed
- Channels used
- Timeline

**Visual support:**
- Before/after examples
- Creative samples
- Campaign imagery

### Section 5: The Results

**Word count:** 100-150 words

Document the outcomes and impact.

**Elements:**
- Quantitative results (numbers, metrics)
- Qualitative results (feedback, recognition)
- Client satisfaction
- Long-term impact

**If metrics are available:**
- [X]% increase in [metric]
- [Y] new [customers/followers/leads]
- [Z] recognition or awards

**If no metrics:**
- Client feedback
- Audience response
- Business outcomes

### Section 6: Client Testimonial

**Word count:** 25-75 words

Include a direct quote from the client.

**Template:**

```
"[Quote about working with Teller and the results achieved.]"

— [Name], [Title], [Company]
```

## Content Collection Questions

### For Client Interview

**Background:**
1. Tell us about your company/brand.
2. What was your situation before working with Teller?
3. What specific challenges were you facing?

**Working with Teller:**
4. Why did you choose Teller?
5. What was the experience like?
6. What surprised you about the process?

**Results:**
7. What changed after the project?
8. What results have you seen?
9. How has this affected your business?

**Recommendation:**
10. Would you recommend Teller? Why?
11. Can we quote you?
