## Homepage Copy Outline

### Hero Section

| Element | Direction | Character Limit |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| Headline | Clear value proposition about storytelling | 50-80 chars |
| Subheadline | Supporting statement about transformation | 100-150 chars |
| Primary CTA | Contact invitation | 20-30 chars |

**Messaging direction:**
- Lead with transformation, not service
- Speak to the desire for authentic connection
- Create curiosity about the approach

**Example directions (not final copy):**
> "Your story is your most powerful asset. Let's discover it together."
> "من قصتك، نبني علامتك."

### Introduction Section

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Headline | What Teller does |
| Body | Brief explanation (50-100 words) |
| CTA | Learn more about us |

### Services Overview

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Section headline | What we offer |
| Per service | Name + 1-2 sentence description |
| CTA | Explore services |

### Featured Work

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Section headline | What we've done |
| Per case study | Title + brief result |
| CTA | See all work |

### Testimonials

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Section headline | What clients say (or similar) |
| Quotes | 2-3 client testimonials |

### Final CTA

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Headline | Ready to start |
| Body | Invitation to conversation |
| CTA | Contact us |

## Services Page Copy Outline

### Introduction

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| Headline | How we help |
| Body | Overview of service philosophy |

### Per Service Section

For each of 5 services:

| Element | Direction | Word Count |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Service name | Clear, descriptive | 2-5 words |
| Description | What it is, what's included | 100-150 words |
| Benefits | Why it matters | 3-5 bullet points |
| CTA | Inquire about service | 15-25 chars |

### Service 1: Storytelling Strategy

**Key messages:**
- Strategic foundation for brand communication
- Discovery and research included
- Deliverables: Brand story, messaging framework

### Service 2: Social Media Management

**Key messages:**
- Story-driven content management
- Excludes filming (clarify scope)
- Consistent brand voice across platforms

### Service 3: Company Profiles

**Key messages:**
- About pages and company profiles
- Story-based approach
- Professional presentation

### Service 4: Storytelling Campaigns

**Key messages:**
- Campaign development for advertising
- Trust-building focus
- Strategy to execution

### Service 5: Treller Training

**Key messages:**
- Internal training program
- Proprietary methodology
- Empower teams with storytelling skills
