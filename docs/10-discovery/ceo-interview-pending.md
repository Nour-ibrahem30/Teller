## Questions for CEO

### Business Foundation
1. How did Teller begin? What's the origin story?
2. What is Teller's mission statement?
3. What are Teller's core values (3-5)?
4. What makes Teller unique vs competitors?

### Target Audience
5. Who is your ideal client? (Industry, size, location)
6. What problems do clients face before working with you?
7. Who makes the buying decision?

### Services
8. What services does Teller offer?
9. What is the Treller framework? How does it work?
10. Is there a flagship/signature service?

### Brand Personality
11. If Teller were a person, how would you describe them?
12. How should Teller sound when communicating?
13. How important is Egyptian/Arabic cultural identity?

### Website Specific
14. Top 3 goals for the website?
15. Primary call-to-action?
16. Arabic/English balance?
17. How often will content be updated?

### Assets
18. Do you have original logo files?
19. Are brand guidelines documented?
20. What photography/case studies are available?

### Business Goals
21. Where do you see Teller in 3-5 years?
22. Who are your main competitors?
23. Budget and timeline for website?
